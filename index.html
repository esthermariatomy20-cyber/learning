Weekly Learning Roadmap

DAY 1

Tokens, Vectors & Data Learning

LLMs process text through Tokenization, where sentences are broken into units (roughly 3/4 of a word). These tokens are converted into numerical Vectors so the computer can understand meaning based on spatial relationships.

Concept: Computers understand that "King" is to "Man" as "Queen" is to "Woman" by calculating the distance between their vectors.
DAY 2

Transformers & MoE Architecture

Modern AIs use Generative Pre-trained Transformer architecture. Advanced versions use Mixture of Experts (MoE), which routes tasks to specialized sub-networks. Only specific "experts" are activated to save energy and time.

DAY 3

Training: RLHF & Constitutional AI

Models are refined through Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). Claude uses Constitutional AI, where the model follows a "constitution" of rules to self-correct and avoid harmful outputs.

DAY 4

RAG & Long Context Windows

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) allows AI to search external databases for live info, reducing hallucinations. A Long Context Window allows the AI to remember thousands of pages in a single conversation.

DAY 5

Advanced Prompt Engineering

Core components include a Role (Persona), Task (Action verb), Context (Background), and Specifics (Formatting). Assigning a clear role can improve model accuracy by up to 20%.

DAY 6

Reasoning & Multimodality

Chain-of-Thought prompting asks the AI to "think step-by-step," boosting logic by 90%. Native Multimodal models process text, audio, images, and video simultaneously.

DAY 7

Enterprise AI & Security

Tools like Writer use Palmyra, a family of secure LLMs built for Finance and Law. Unlike consumer AIs, these guarantee that private company data is never used to train future public models.
