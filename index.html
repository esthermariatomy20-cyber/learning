Weekly Learning Roadmap

DAY 1

Tokens, Vectors & Data Learning

LLMs process text through Tokenization, where sentences are broken into units (roughly 3/4 of a word). These tokens are converted into numerical Vectors (mathematical lists) so the computer can understand meaning based on spatial relationships between concepts.

Concept: Computers understand that "King" is to "Man" as "Queen" is to "Woman" by calculating the distance between their vectors.
DAY 2

Transformers & MoE Architecture

Modern AIs like ChatGPT use Generative Pre-trained Transformer architecture. Advanced versions use Mixture of Experts (MoE), which routes tasks to specialized sub-networks. Instead of running the whole model, only specific "experts" (like a math or translation expert) are activated to save energy and time.

DAY 3

Training: RLHF & Constitutional AI

Models are refined through Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), where humans rank answers to improve safety. Claude uses Constitutional AI, where the model follows a "constitution" of rules to self-correct and avoid harmful outputs autonomously.

DAY 4

RAG & Long Context Windows

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) allows AI to search external databases for live info, reducing "hallucinations." Meanwhile, a Long Context Window (like Gemini's 2 million tokens) allows the AI to remember thousands of pages of text in a single conversation.

DAY 5

Advanced Prompt Engineering

The practice of crafting inputs for better accuracy. Core components include a Role (Persona), Task (Action verb), Context (Background), and Specifics (Formatting). Assigning a clear role can improve model accuracy by up to 20%.

DAY 6

Reasoning & Multimodality

Chain-of-Thought prompting asks the AI to "think step-by-step," boosting logic on complex problems by 90%. Native Multimodal models (like Gemini) process text, audio, images, and video simultaneously in one unified architecture.

DAY 7

Enterprise AI & Security

Tools like Writer use Palmyra, a family of secure LLMs built specifically for high-security industries like Finance and Law. Unlike consumer AIs, these guarantee that private company data is never used to train future public models.
